How it works

From raw data to a signal

No setup. No account. Open the app and follow four steps.

  1. 01

    Pick a token

    Browse the top 100 or search. Data is aggregated from CoinGecko, Binance, Bybit, OKX, MEXC, Hyperliquid and DexScreener.

  2. 02

    Read the chart

    Candlesticks with EMA 20/50, Bollinger Bands, RSI and MACD. Switch timeframe from 5M to 1W.

  3. 03

    Tap analyze

    Choose SAFE for cautious setups or DEGEN for aggressive ones. The AI returns direction, entry, target, stop and risk/reward.

  4. 04

    Track the signal

    A validity timer, entry-zone monitor, and invalidation and reversal alerts keep the call honest. History is logged.
