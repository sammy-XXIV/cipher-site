FAQ

Common questions

Check here first. The answer may be quick.

Prices or charts are not loading

CIPHER pulls live data from a server and several exchanges. Wait a few seconds and refresh. If it keeps failing, message us with the token and the time it happened.

The AI analysis failed or took too long

Analysis runs on a server and can fail when it is busy. Tap analyze again. If it keeps failing, tell us which token and timeframe you used.

Is a signal a guarantee?

No. Signals are AI-generated for education and are not financial advice. Markets are risky, so do your own research.

What do SAFE and DEGEN mean?

They are the two analysis modes. SAFE aims for more cautious setups. DEGEN aims for more aggressive ones.

Where does the market data come from?

CoinGecko, Binance, Bybit, OKX, MEXC, Hyperliquid and DexScreener.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. CIPHER is free, with no login and no keys in your browser.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The app has a mobile layout and a desktop layout.
